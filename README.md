## About
Our coral reefs are dying at an alarming rate, yet far too few people understand just how critical they are. Often called the “rainforests of the sea,” coral reefs support nearly a quarter of all marine life despite covering only 1% of the ocean floor. Their role in maintaining ocean health is profound — they help prevent coastal erosion, reduce the impact of storms, and provide food and livelihoods for millions around the world. Before working on this project, I had little understanding of just how essential reefs are, and I suspect many others are in the same boat. That’s why raising awareness, providing tangible ways to help, and backing it with real data are more important now than ever.

## Story Telling Strategies Used
I wanted to make sure the audience had a good understanding of coral reefs and their importance before looking at the data, so I structured my slides to be very informational. I also wanted the Dash app to be easily navigable so that when a user interacted with it, they could easily see the data they wished to explore. Having two line graphs side by side makes it very easy for the user to visually compare two pieces of data and see correlations between them. Having a stacked bar graph, with yellow indicating low severity and red indicating high severity, allows the user to quickly see what is bad and what is good. It also allows them to easily identify the majority category per year.
